      Model: 22 PHCs, 6 CHCs, 1 District Hospital over 70 × 70 km. Today, the patient goes to the nearest centre and is referred on (20 min each time) if it has no antivenom or no doctor. With VishRaksha, the ambulance goes straight to the nearest centre that confirmed ready; 5% of "ready" answers are wrong on arrival. Code: simulation/simulate.py (seed 7). These are planning numbers, not field results.

      Why 108 alone is not enough

      • 108 takes the patient to the nearest government centre. It cannot see whether that centre has antivenom tonight or a doctor on duty.
      • India has about 58,000 snakebite deaths a year (Million Death Study). Many patients reach a centre that cannot treat them and lose an hour in referral.
      • VishRaksha does not replace 108. 108 moves the patient; the agent picks and readies the centre and tells the driver where to go.
      • Nobody in the family opens an app. The trigger is the 108 call, and centres only need a phone that can take a call.

      Pilot plan

      • One district, one monsoon (June to September), with the District Health Office.
      • One metric: minutes from the 108 call to arrival at a centre that gives antivenom.
      • Running cost estimate: about ₹1.5–2 per call minute for voice + WhatsApp; under ₹50,000 per district per monsoon.

      Safety controls

      • No diagnosis and no treatment advice. The agent only routes and reminds; doctors decide.
      • Human-in-the-loop: unclear location goes to the control room; moving stock needs approval; every centre declining alerts the District officer.
      • Ambulance never waits for the agent. It leaves at once; the destination reaches it on the way.
      • Minimal data: no ID number, masked phone numbers, logs deleted after the case closes (DPDP Act 2023).

      Known limitations

      • Real duty numbers and the 108 link need permission from the state; this demo simulates them.
      • Stock is only as fresh as the morning check-in, so every call re-confirms it.
      • Dialect speech can fail; keypad input and a human operator are the fallbacks.
